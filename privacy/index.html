Barpath privacy policy

Effective November 13, 2026. Developer: Dan Vallier. Support: vallierapps@gmail.com

All policies Support

Short version. Barpath is a one-time-purchase app. We collect no data.

Who we are

Barpath is published by Dan Vallier (Vallier Apps). Contact: vallierapps@gmail.com.

What the app is

Barpath is a personal gym log for sets, reps, weight, routines, history, and barbell plate math. It is not medical advice and does not promise results.

Data we collect

We collect no data. There is no account, no analytics SDK, and no advertising identifier. Nothing is uploaded to us.

Where your data lives

Workouts, routines, lifts, and personal records are stored only on this device, in the app's private storage. Barpath does not use iCloud or any server. They are included in your device's own backups if you have those turned on.

Permissions

Barpath does not request Location, Photos, Camera, Health, Microphone, or Tracking permissions.

Third parties

There are no third-party analytics, advertising, or crash SDKs. We do not sell or share your information.

Exports, backups, and deletion

If you export a CSV, you choose where it goes (Files, AirDrop, Mail, and so on). Export is optional and only happens when you start it.

Delete a workout or routine in the app, or delete the app, to remove what Barpath stores on that device.

Children

Barpath is not directed at children under 13 and is not in the Kids Category.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted on this page and in the app. The in-app copy is the current policy until this public page is later updated.

Contact

Questions about privacy: vallierapps@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated. The copy inside the Barpath app is always the current policy until a newer public page is posted here.
